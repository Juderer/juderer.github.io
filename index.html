I am a postdoctoral researcher in Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences, working with Professor Wensheng Wang.

Prior to joining AIRCAS, I obtained my Ph.D. degree in 2025 from Beijing Jiaotong University, under the supervision of Professor Ruipeng Gao. My dissertation work focused on indoor localization and mapping via mobile crowdsourcing, with relevant findings published in ACM MobiCom, IEEE TMC, and ACM IMWUT/UbiComp.

My research interests include vehicle dead reckoning, indoor localization, and indoor mapping. Now I focus on Graph Structure Learning and Knowledge Graph.
